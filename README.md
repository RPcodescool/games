Animal Go: Battle to be the best 
 
  A game with different areas, and animals. Each area has some animals. You can buy balls with coins and use balls to capture animals. After you capture the animals, you can put them on your team. On your team, if you click Challenge Wild Battle, one of the animals on your team will fight a random wild animals with its abilities. One example is that you capture a polar bear, and it chose to fight a lion. You can either flee, heal or use one of its atacks like Arctic Swipe. Each time you win, you get coins and some exp for levels.

  In the animaldex, you can see all the animals you have captured. There are 6 spots on your team. There is also a profile page, where you can reset the game, see how many coins you have, see how many battles you have won and battled, how many animals you have caught, and your trainer exp for levels.
